The Famous Five: Five on a treasure Island

Author: Enid Blyton

Price: $75

The story begins with Julian, Dick, Anne, and George (Georgina) holidaying near the seaside; they are joined by Timmy, George’s loyal dog. The children discover hints about a hidden treasure on the nearby Kirrin Island, which belongs to George’s family. The Jungle Book

Author: Rudyard Kipling

Price: $76

The Jungle Book is a celebrated 1894 collection of stories by English author Rudyard Kipling, set mostly in the vibrant forests of India. The most famous portion of the book centers on Mowgli, an orphaned human "man-cub" who is adopted and raised by a pack of wolves. Guided by the wise, sleepy brown bear Baloo and the fiercely protective black panther Bagheera, Mowgli learns the strict "Law of the Jungle." However, he constantly faces danger from Shere Khan, a ruthless Bengal tiger determined to kill him, forcing Mowgli to eventually confront his identity and choose between the world of wild beasts and the world of humans.
